## 💡 Tips for Maximum Profit

1. **Quality Matters**: Focus on improving soil and care to achieve higher quality ratings
2. **Seasonal Planning**: Plan crop rotations to maximize seasonal profits
3. **Processing Advantage**: Processed goods (cheese, mayonnaise) often more profitable than raw materials
4. **Mining Strategy**: Deep floors yield more valuable ores and gems
5. **Tree Investment**: Long-term investment but consistent seasonal income
6. **Animal Care**: Well-cared animals produce higher quality products
